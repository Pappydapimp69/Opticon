How to play

As a Prisoner you win by reaching the gate yourself. As the Watcher you win by catching every prisoner — one of them getting out is a loss. Play either side, alone or 2-player.

  • Prisoner — Move up to 3 tiles/turn (WASD / arrows). One step is quiet; 2+ tiles in a turn leaves noise behind — a red pulse the Watcher can see. A red screen edge + heartbeat means the Watcher's gaze covers you RIGHT NOW while you're lit or noisy — that's the danger signal, not the text log. Break a window for a loud shortcut or distraction (Shift + direction). You run with two AI companions who move on their own — but only your own escape wins the game. They can draw the eye, break ground ahead of you, or get taken; they cannot carry you out. Reach the gate yourself or you lose.
  • Watcher — You never see prisoners directly, only noise pings and light. Aim your 90° gaze with Q/E — it only previews, so you can switch sides or cancel; Space confirms it (you get one rotation a turn). Bluff a claimed direction (1–4) to mislead, then Space again to scan & end your turn. On a gamepad the d-pad matches the screen — up is the top of the map as drawn, not a fixed compass point. Anyone caught in your TRUE gaze while lit or noisy is captured.
  • Items — each kind has its own colour and shape on the floor: 🔔 gold sphere (Distraction), 🧣 violet ring (Muffle), 🗝️ blue spike (Lockpick), ✂️ orange shard (Cutters). Walk over one to carry it (2 max). Press 1/2 to use (gamepad: LT cycles, RT fires). The caption under your item chips always says what the highlighted item does and what to press next — 🔔, 🗝️ and ✂️ are aimed, so they arm first and then want a direction; 🧣 and 🪶 spend the instant you press them. 🔔 Distraction — arm it, press a direction to throw a fake noise that way. 🧣 Muffle — this turn's movement makes no noise. 🗝️ Lockpick — arm, press a direction to open that door free. ✂️ Cutters — arm, press a direction at a switch to kill those lights for good. 🪶 Golden Feather — plucked from the griffin: spend it and the Gaze readout shows the eye's TRUE facing for that round, instead of "?". It costs no movement — the price is that you found only one, and knowing where the eye points is worth nothing if you have no moves left to use it.
  • Watcher skills — always shown in your skill bar, with cooldowns and situational requirements (keys 5–9; on a gamepad LT cycles the slot and RT activates it). Dim skills explain what they still need when pressed. 🎭 Double bluff — make one claim, activate it, then choose a different direction for the second. 🔦 Wide scan — arm a 180° sweep for your next scan, at the cost of bluffing that turn. 📡 Echo memory — refresh every active noise trace. 🔒 Remote lock — slam the nearest eligible open door shut. 🚨 Dispatch guards — activate it, then choose a quadrant to send a pair of guards hunting there.
  • Guards — unlike the tower's gaze, a dispatched pair is physical, and it is short-winded. Each guard sees only one square in every direction (including diagonals) — walk two squares wide of one and it never knew you were there. Each carries an action bar of 7 points: for its first 2 turns it sprints up to 3 squares for a single point, and after that every square costs a point, so it slows to a walk. Grabbing you costs 3 points. When the bar hits zero the guard is recalled the next turn — its glow dims as it runs down, so you can see how much chase it has left. They chase the freshest noise in their quadrant; a 🔔 Distraction pulls them to the noise instead of you. As a Prisoner, your Zone readout names the quadrant you're standing in and turns orange when a squad is sweeping it — that's how you tell whether "guards dispatched to the North quadrant" means you.
  • Captured vs. escaped — a captured prisoner is out; the Watcher wins once every prisoner is caught — or once the round limit runs out (the counter turns red near the end). Any one prisoner reaching the gate is a Prisoner win.
  • Views — Press V / Tab to cycle Prisoner / Watcher / Overview. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Intro cutscene — a camera flythrough plays at the start of each game; press anything to skip it.
