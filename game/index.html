How to play
  • Prisoner — Move up to 3 tiles/turn (WASD / arrows). A single step is quiet; moving 2+ tiles reveals the tile you started on as noise. Glass always makes noise. Reach the glowing green gate to escape.
  • Watcher — You sit in the tower and never see prisoners directly. Rotate your 90° gaze (Q/E), bluff a second direction (1–4) to spread paranoia, then press Space to scan & end your turn. A prisoner caught in your true gaze while lit or making noise is captured.
  • Views — Press V / Tab to cycle Prisoner / Watcher / Overview. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
