How to play

Prisoners escape through the gate; the Watcher tries to catch one in its gaze. Play either side, alone or 2-player.

  • Prisoner — Move up to 3 tiles/turn (WASD / arrows). One step is quiet; 2+ tiles in a turn leaves noise behind — a red pulse the Watcher can see. A red screen edge + heartbeat means the Watcher's gaze covers you RIGHT NOW while you're lit or noisy — that's the danger signal, not the text log. Break a window for a loud shortcut or distraction (Shift + direction). You lead a group of 3 — teammates move on their own.
  • Watcher — You never see prisoners directly, only noise pings and light. Rotate your 90° gaze (Q/E), bluff a second claimed direction (1–4) to mislead, then Space to scan & end your turn. Anyone caught in your TRUE gaze while lit or noisy is captured.
  • Items — each kind has its own colour and shape on the floor: 🔔 gold sphere (Distraction), 🧣 violet ring (Muffle), 🗝️ blue spike (Lockpick), ✂️ orange shard (Cutters). Walk over one to carry it (2 max). Press 1/2 to use (gamepad: LT cycles, RT fires). 🔔 Distraction — arm it, press a direction to throw a fake noise that way. 🧣 Muffle — this turn's movement makes no noise. 🗝️ Lockpick — arm, press a direction to open that door free. ✂️ Cutters — arm, press a direction at a switch to kill those lights for good.
  • Watcher skills — always shown in your skill bar, with cooldowns and situational requirements (keys 5–9; on a gamepad LT cycles the slot and RT activates it). Dim skills explain what they still need when pressed. 🎭 Double bluff — make one claim, activate it, then choose a different direction for the second. 🔦 Wide scan — arm a 180° sweep for your next scan, at the cost of bluffing that turn. 📡 Echo memory — refresh every active noise trace. 🔒 Remote lock — slam the nearest eligible open door shut. 🚨 Dispatch guards — activate it, then choose a quadrant to send a pair of guards hunting there.
  • Guards — unlike the tower's gaze, a dispatched pair is physical: they walk the map (faster than you can run), chase the freshest noise in their quadrant, and catch you on plain line of sight — so cover breaks them where it can't break the gaze. They give up after a few rounds. As a Prisoner, your Zone readout names the quadrant you're standing in and turns orange when a squad is sweeping it — that's how you tell whether "guards dispatched to the North quadrant" means you. Throwing a 🔔 Distraction pulls them to the noise instead of you.
  • Captured vs. escaped — a captured prisoner is out; the Watcher wins once every prisoner is caught — or once the round limit runs out (the counter turns red near the end). Any one prisoner reaching the gate is a Prisoner win.
  • Views — Press V / Tab to cycle Prisoner / Watcher / Overview. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Intro cutscene — a camera flythrough plays at the start of each game; press anything to skip it.
